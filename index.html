Lentra DTT

Lentra Daily Time Tracker. A mobile app for Lentra employees to log daily development and miscellaneous effort into DevRev from their phone.

The app is distributed internally to Lentra staff. It stores your settings and history on your own device in an encrypted database, keeps your access tokens in the device keychain, and can, if you choose, back up an encrypted copy to a Google Drive account you connect.
